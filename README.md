# Kambradu

**Learn something useful. Connect it to your life. Keep what matters.**

Kambradu is an independent web prototype for learning Kristang. It brings a small learning moment and a personal memory together.

## A personal beginning

The idea grew from my own attempts to learn languages, questions about family and my grandparents, and a visit to Melaka in 2013.

I had found a Kristang greeting, but I still had a basic question: who could I say it to?

That question stayed with me. A word becomes easier to remember when it is connected to a person, a place or a moment that matters.

## Why this can be difficult

Learning any language takes time. It can be harder when speakers are far away, learning material is scarce or academic, the language is mainly spoken, or it is difficult to know what to practise next.

Kambradu starts with one modest response: make the next learning step clear, then give the learner somewhere to keep the context that makes it meaningful.

## How the prototype works

There are three places:

- **Today** offers one next step.
- **Learn** contains two dictionary-listed Kristang words.
- **Memories** keeps words and notes in the current browser.

Each learning moment follows the same rhythm:

`Meet -> Meaning -> Recall -> Connect -> Keep`

The learner sees the word, checks its meaning, recalls it in writing, connects it to their life, then chooses whether to keep it.

## What works now

- text learning for `sabang` and `janela`;
- immediate written feedback;
- `Again`, `Almost` and `Got it` review choices;
- one due review at a time;
- browser-saved memories;
- edit, practise, delete, export, restore and clear-all controls;
- recovery options when stored data cannot be read;
- responsive keyboard-friendly screens;
- a release status endpoint at `/status`.

## What is not available

There is no reviewed audio, pronunciation teaching, account, cloud sync, upload, public contribution, sharing or community publishing.

Memories are saved in the current browser profile. Other people using that profile may be able to see them. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

Kambradu is not endorsed or linguistically approved by a Kristang community body. The two current entries have been checked against a published dictionary. They are not presented as speaker attested or partner reviewed.

## Sources and community authority

The current entries cite Alan N. Baxter and Patrick de Silva, *A Dictionary of Kristang (Malacca Creole Portuguese)*, Pacific Linguistics, The Australian National University, 2005, online edition 2015, [DOI 10.15144/PL-564.cover](https://doi.org/10.15144/PL-564.cover), CC BY-SA 4.0.

Future teaching material requires a named reviewer who can confirm contemporary use, preferred form and lesson suitability. Language naming, permissions, access and publication rules must follow direct community guidance.
