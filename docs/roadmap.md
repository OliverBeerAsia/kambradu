# Roadmap

## Current public prototype

The active milestone is one honest text-learning loop and learner-controlled browser memories.

Implemented locally:

- Today, Learn and Memories navigation;
- `sabang` and `janela` only;
- Meet, Meaning, Recall, Connect and Keep;
- lesson-scoped session recovery and ISO review dates;
- Again, Almost and Got it scheduling;
- empty fresh profiles;
- edit, practise, delete, export, restore and clear-all controls;
- malformed-data recovery;
- production closure of advanced routes;
- release metadata and demo-auth build guards.

Still required before release:

- production fingerprinting;
- preview deployment from an exact commit;
- production-server browser tests;
- accessibility scan and manual assistive-technology checks;
- preview screenshots and human copy review;
- promotion of the verified preview artifact;
- live release-ID and route verification;
- rollback confirmation.

## Partnership gates

The following require a named community relationship and agreed rules before design or implementation:

- additional teaching material;
- contemporary usage guidance;
- pronunciation and audio;
- language naming and variation;
- consent and withdrawal;
- uploads or contributions;
- community review and publication;
- access rules for sensitive material.

## Later technical work

After partnership and governance gates are met:

- accounts and secure sync;
- revocable consent records;
- governed audio storage and deletion;
- role and permission design;
- reliable offline behaviour;
- multi-device restore;
- audited publication workflows.

No multi-language expansion is planned within the current milestone.
